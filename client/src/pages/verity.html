The transformation

Financial data is rarely wrong because nobody cares. It becomes unreliable because the work behind it is fragmented across tables, messages, reviewers and memory.

Confidence does not come from data.It comes from how data becomes trusted.

01 / the hidden risk

A datapoint is never just a number.

Behind a published value sit analysts, source documents, judgement, discussion, correction, review and distribution. The previous workflow reduced that chain to a basic table—making the most important context invisible.

Project summary

Verity Grid

Industry: Financial intelligence and enterprise data operations.

Users: Research analysts, data teams, reviewers and workflow owners.

Problem: A basic table captured values but not the evidence, discussion, accountability or distribution behind them.

What we made: A connected feedback and data-confidence system that combines task visibility, behavioural history, contextual review, correction, approvals and downstream handoff.

Design contribution: Product framing, information architecture, interaction model, workflow design, visual system and product storytelling.
